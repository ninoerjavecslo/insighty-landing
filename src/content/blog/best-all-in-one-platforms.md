### 2. Monday.com — Flexible Work OS for Agencies

Monday.com is a flexible Work Operating System that agencies use for project management, CRM, and workflow automation. Thanks to its highly customizable boards and integrations, it can be configured as an all-in-one platform.

**Key Features:**

- Customizable project workflows and dashboards.
- Time tracking and workload management.
- CRM and pipeline management modules.
- Automation recipes for recurring tasks and notifications.
- Integration with finance tools for invoicing via third-party apps.

**Pros:**

- Highly flexible and widely adopted.
- Large ecosystem of integrations.
- Visual dashboards simplify complex project tracking.

**Cons:**

- Requires significant configuration to become an all-in-one solution.
- Financial and invoicing features are not native and need add-ons.
- Can be overwhelming for smaller teams without dedicated admins.

**Pricing:**

- Basic: $12/user/month
- Standard: $20/user/month
- Pro: $35/user/month (recommended for agencies needing automation and integration)
