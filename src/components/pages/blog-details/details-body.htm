Why Your Agency Needs a Single Source of Truth: Meet Insighty

Nino Erjavec

April 24, 2026 • 5 min read

Introduction

If you run a digital agency, you know the pain of scattered data. You have 50+ clients across active projects, website maintenance retainers, hosting, and domain management. Each client has a different contract. Some are fixed-fee milestone projects. Some are hourly. Some are monthly retainers with change requests. Some just pay for yearly hosting.

As an agency owner or project manager, you need to report to management monthly, prepare invoice planning, and forecast revenue. But when you dive into the data, you hit a wall. To answer a simple question like "What is the full financial status of Client X?", you have to open Jira, check Productive, dig through a hosting spreadsheet, cross-reference a domain spreadsheet, and read through email threads. The information exists, but it is scattered everywhere. There is no single source of truth.

This fragmentation leads to real business problems:

  • Invoice leakage: You forget to invoice completed work because someone left the company or a contract expired.
  • Unreliable forecasts: Your pipeline deals all look the same in spreadsheets, making cash flow planning a guessing game.
  • Wasted time: You spend 5+ hours every month just searching across SharePoint and documents to figure out what is in a contract.
  • Manual reporting: Assembling monthly maintenance reports takes hours of pulling ticket counts from Jira and checking hours against contracts.

You have probably tried every CRM and task management tool out there. But none of them have the specific features needed for a digital agency with a complex mix of services. They are either too generic or too expensive. That is why Insighty was built.

What is Insighty?

Insighty is an internal AI agency management intelligence tool designed specifically for digital agencies. It is a single operational platform that connects every client, project, maintenance contract, hosting plan, invoice, domain, and pipeline deal into one unified dashboard.

It replaces fragmented tools and manual work with one source of truth that is live, linked, and actionable.

Core Features for Agency Operations

Insighty is built around the realities of running a digital agency. Here is how it solves the most common operational headaches:

1. Comprehensive Revenue Planning

Insighty handles the complex mix of agency revenue streams. Whether it is fixed-fee projects, monthly maintenance retainers, hosting revenue, or consulting, Insighty tracks it all. The Revenue Planner provides a multi-month view of all planned revenue, structured by client and project. You can see planned amounts, actual amounts, status badges, and probability indicators for every active revenue source.

2. "This Month" Action View

The most important view in Insighty is the "This Month" dashboard. It shows every invoice that should be issued this month, regardless of type — maintenance retainer rows, project milestone rows, monthly and yearly hosting rows, and domain renewal rows. Project managers can easily confirm what has been issued and what is still pending, eliminating invoice leakage.

3. Automated Maintenance and Hosting Management

No more manual monthly maintenance reports. Insighty tracks active maintenance contracts, hosting margins, and domain expiries. It alerts you when items are expiring in the next 30 days, reducing reputational and operational risks before they become problems.

4. Intelligent Forecasting

Insighty brings probability weighting to your pipeline. It distinguishes between a signed contract and a verbal agreement, making your revenue forecasts and cash flow planning much more reliable. You get a weighted view of expected revenue alongside the optimistic and conservative scenarios — all from a single screen.

5. AI-Powered Efficiency

With its Pixel Intelligence layer, Insighty uses AI to streamline operations. It can generate full proposals — from brief to structured PDF with pricing, scope, and team details — in a fraction of the time it takes to write them manually. The built-in AI assistant also lets team members ask natural language questions about clients, revenue, and forecasts without navigating the dashboard at all.

The ROI of a Unified Platform

The manual overhead of managing a digital agency with scattered tools is significant. Invoice leakage, manual reporting, and proposal writing can cost an agency 50–60 hours a month of senior PM and admin time. That is the equivalent of one part-time hire, permanently.

By centralizing operations into a single platform, Insighty eliminates this overhead. It stops revenue from leaking through uninvoiced work, provides reliable forecasts, and frees up your team to focus on delivering great work rather than hunting down spreadsheets.

If you are tired of opening five different tools just to understand the financial status of a client, it is time to look at a unified solution. Insighty provides the operational intelligence digital agencies need to scale efficiently and profitably.
